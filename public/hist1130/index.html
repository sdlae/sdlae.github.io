Welcome to Shannen's HIST 1130 Unessay Project! 👋🏼

This project is a series of three jigsaw puzzles that break down how colonization shaped the Philippines, literally piece by piece. Each puzzle represents a different era of foreign rule, and together, they tell the story of how outside forces pulled the country in different directions, only for it to become something entirely its own.

From Spanish missions to American public schools to Japanese occupation during WWII, each stage left behind a different mark. This isn’t just about history. It’s about how those legacies still echo today in culture, politics, religion, and identity.
